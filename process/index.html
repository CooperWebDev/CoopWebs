How it works

Four simple steps

No contracts and no jargon. The same four steps for a bot, a site or a rank boost.

01

Send your request

Pick what you want on the order page and fill in the short form: a Discord bot, website, Twitch bot or a Rocket League boost.

02

Agree the price

We reply on Discord. For builds we confirm the Steam card value, for boosts we agree the price and how you pay.

03

We build or boost

Once everything is agreed we get to work as fast as we can. Quality is never rushed. For boosts, we ask for the account login on Discord.

04

You get it back

We send builds as a zip file on Discord. Boosted accounts are handed straight back to you.
